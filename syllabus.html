Syllabus

Schedule

09:00 - 09:30 | Welcome, Introductions, and Framing

  • Introductions
  • Short exercise: tracing the data behind a real policy decision
  • Why traditional "download -> clean -> analyze" workflows break down

09:30 - 10:30 | Lecture 1: From Planetary Problems to Data Infrastructures

Big data as infrastructure, not just scale

  • The mismatch: planetary-scale problems vs local data workflows
  • Where data live: platforms, APIs, cloud systems

10:30 - 10:45 | Break

10:45 - 11:45 | Group Work 1: Framing Questions and Finding Data

Focus: discovery, scale, and feasibility

  • Form groups (assigned themes or self-proposed topics)
  • Define a policy question
  • Identify:
  • What data are needed
  • Where those data exist (platforms, repositories, APIs, catalogs)
  • Whether the scale of the data matches the scale of the problem

11:45 - 12:00 | Regroup and Reflection

12:00 - 13:00 | Lunch

13:00 - 14:00 | Lecture 2: Accessing Data Where They Live

Cloud-native computing in practice

  • Working with data you cannot download
  • Querying distributed datasets (APIs, cloud platforms)
  • Introduction to STAC catalogs for geospatial data access

14:00 - 15:00 | Group Work 2: Accessing and Working with Data

Focus: implementation and friction

15:00 - 15:15 | Break

15:15 - 15:45 | Mini-Lecture: Data Disparities and Infrastructures

  • Uneven data landscapes
  • Insights from Unicorns, Show Ponies, and Gazelles Reading
  • How infrastructures shape access, knowledge, and power

15:45 - 16:30 | Group Work 3

Focus: systems thinking and implications

16:30 - 17:00 | Presentations and Closing Reflections

  • Group presentations (5 minutes each)
  • Discussion:
  • Closing reflection: What does it mean to do policy in a world of planetary-scale, uneven, and infrastructure-dependent data?
